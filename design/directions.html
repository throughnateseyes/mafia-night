Three ways Mafia Night could look

Same game, same drawings, three different personalities. Each shows the home screen, joining, and your role reveal. Pick one (or mix: “A’s type with C’s night glow”) and I’ll carry it through every screen, the narrator phone and the TV.

A

Camp journal my pick

Like a scrapbook someone kept all weekend. Handwritten titles, taped polaroids, a trail-map behind everything, and your role arrives as a stamped ticket. Warm, personal, clearly made by hand for Steph.

TexturePaper grain plus faint contour lines of the lake, like a hiking map.

TypeKalam (hand lettering) for titles, Reenie Beanie for scribbled notes, Karla for reading.

DrawingsSame flat art, with gently wobbling hand-cut edges and no outlines.

B

Park poster

A vintage national-park print. Two inks, a slightly off-register orange shadow, halftone dots, an arched poster frame, and your role as an embroidered ranger patch. Bold and graphic, reads great on the TV.

TexturePrint speckle in the art, halftone dots fading up from the bottom.

TypeBevan (chunky slab, like park signage) with Public Sans.

DrawingsRiso-printed: speckled fills and an orange misprint shadow.

C

Ghost story

The whole game happens around the campfire at night. Dark sky with stars, a warm glow coming up from the bottom of the screen, sketchy chalk lettering, and your role lit up like a card under a flashlight. Most atmospheric, especially with the lights off.

TextureStar speckle, film grain and a flickering firelight glow.

TypeCabin Sketch (literally) for titles, Reenie Beanie notes, Nunito Sans.

DrawingsArt fades softly into the dark, like it is lit by the fire.
